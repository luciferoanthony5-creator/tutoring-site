    About this site

    AntsPhysicsLessons is a free, open-access collection of video lessons and printable worksheets. There is nothing to sign up for.

    The lessons are made by Anthony, a mechanical engineering undergraduate who works as a tutor and learning assistant. I am not a teacher or a professor. I am a student who has spent a lot of hours helping other students through this material, and these are the explanations that worked.

    Every video and worksheet here is original. Worksheets and answer keys are free to print and use in class. If you find a mistake, please tell me; fixing it helps the next person.
